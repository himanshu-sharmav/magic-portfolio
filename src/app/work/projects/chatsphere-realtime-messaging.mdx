## What I explored

I built a real-time messaging prototype with Django Channels, WebSocket room broadcasts, and persisted message history.

The prototype helped me explore asynchronous message handling and the boundary between transient connections and stored conversation data. Its scope is room broadcasts and persisted history; production access controls and load testing remain further work.
