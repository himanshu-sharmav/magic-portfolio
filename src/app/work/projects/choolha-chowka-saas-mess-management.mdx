## The product

Choolha Chowka is a meal-subscription platform covering account access, carts, plan durations, leave handling, payments, and subscription changes.

## My ownership

I owned the backend and DevOps work, collaborating with Abhimanyu Singh on the frontend. The backend uses Django REST Framework, PostgreSQL, Redis, Celery, Razorpay, and Docker.

## A payment is more than a browser callback

Browser confirmation can fail after a payment succeeds. I implemented HMAC-verified Razorpay webhooks to reconcile payment state and activate subscriptions independently of that browser response.

The handling includes repeat-payment checks and isolates notification failures so an email error does not undo the subscription flow.

## Other workflows I built

- Multi-role OTP authentication and Redis OTP caching.
- Multi-plan carts, configurable durations, leave handling, and subscription-day changes.
- Celery jobs for expiry and email processing, including retry behavior.
- Docker deployment support for the backend and supporting services.
