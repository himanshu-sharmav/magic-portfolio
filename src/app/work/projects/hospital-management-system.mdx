## The project

A hospital appointment and queue application built with React, Node.js/Express, TypeScript, PostgreSQL/Prisma, and Python.

## My contributions

I built appointment and queue workflows with role-based access, and integrated face-recognition attendance, doctor mapping, automatic attendance closure, and queue updates.

The AI integration loads a trained no-show model, applies its preprocessing, and connects inference to capacity, wait-list, and slot-scheduling logic.

## Engineering focus

Attendance changes the active queue, doctor mapping affects scheduling, and model output feeds the capacity and wait-list rules. I connected these pieces into the application workflow.

I built this as a final-year engineering project. My AI contribution was integrating an existing trained model into the scheduling application. Clinical validation and model-accuracy evaluation were outside this project’s scope.
